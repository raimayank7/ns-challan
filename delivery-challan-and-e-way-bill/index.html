Delivery challan and e-way bill

Goods moving on a challan follow the same e-way bill rules as goods moving on an invoice. The challan is the document you name on the e-way bill, so the two must match exactly.

When an e-way bill is needed

  • Value over ₹50,000: when a consignment worth more than ₹50,000 moves by a motor vehicle, an e-way bill is needed, whether it goes on an invoice or a challan. The value includes GST. Some states use a higher limit for movement within the state, so check your state's limit.
  • Job work to another state: when a principal sends goods to a job worker in another state, the e-way bill is needed whatever the value. Either the principal or the registered job worker makes it.
  • A few goods and modes of transport are exempt. Your accountant or the e-way bill portal's help pages list them.

This is why the challan should show a value even when no tax is charged: the e-way bill asks for it.

From the challan to the e-way bill

The e-way bill is made on the government portal (ewaybillgst.gov.in) or its app. Type in these details from your challan:

E-way bill fieldFrom the challan
Transaction typeOutward (you are sending)
Sub typeJob work, For own use, Exhibition or fairs, SKD/CKD/lots, Others… matching the purpose
Document typeDelivery Challan
Document number and dateDC/26-27/021, 4 Nov 2026
From and ToConsignor and consignee: GSTIN, address, PIN code
ItemsDescription, HSN, quantity, taxable value, tax rate
Part BVehicle number, or transporter ID and LR / docket number

Write the e-way bill number in the challan's Notes and print again, so the copy with the driver carries both.

How long it is valid

For normal cargo the e-way bill is valid for 1 day for up to 200 km, and 1 more day for every further 200 km or part of it. Over-dimensional cargo gets less distance per day. A day ends at midnight, so an e-way bill made in the evening loses most of its first day. If a vehicle breaks down, update Part B with the new vehicle number before it moves on.

A worked example

A Ludhiana unit sends yarn worth ₹1,40,000 to a dyer in Panipat, Haryana, about 280 km away, on DC/26-27/021. It is job work to another state, so an e-way bill is needed (it would be needed here even for ₹20,000). Sub type Job work, document type Delivery Challan, Part B vehicle PB10 CD 5678. 280 km means 2 days of validity. The dyer returns the yarn on their own challan with a fresh e-way bill.

Common mistakes

  • Challan number on the e-way bill typed differently from the printed challan (an extra space or a missing slash).
  • Value on the e-way bill without tax when the challan shows tax, or the other way round.
  • Vehicle changed on the road but Part B not updated.
In the maker, fill vehicle number, transporter and LR number before printing, and tick GST if the goods are moving for a supply, so the challan shows the same value and tax as the e-way bill. For goods sold on a tax invoice, use the invoice maker.
